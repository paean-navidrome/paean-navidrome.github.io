Paean Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Paean doesn't collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app connects only to the music server you choose, and everything it saves stays on your device.

What Paean stores on your device

  • Your server's address and your user name.
  • A sign-in token that Paean creates from your password and a random salt, as the Subsonic protocol requires. Paean never stores your password itself.
  • Your settings, such as your theme, Home layout and equalizer profiles.
  • Music you download, a cache of recently played songs and cover art, and the last song you played, so the app reopens where you left off.

Paean doesn't send any of this to us or to any third party. It only sends your sign-in details to your own server, so the server can verify you.

Removing your data

  • iPhone: Deleting the app removes all of this data, including your downloads and settings.
  • Mac: macOS keeps an app's data after you delete the app. To remove it, delete the Paean folder in ~/Library/Containers.

Who Paean connects to

  • Your music server (Navidrome or another Subsonic-compatible server). Paean uses it to browse, stream and download your music. It also saves your likes, playlists, play history and play queue there. Your server's own policies apply to that data.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a free public directory of radio stations, but only when you use Radio. Paean sends it a station's name or address to look up that station's details and logo. The radio streams themselves play directly from each station.
  • Your other devices running Paean, if you use them together (for example, playing music on your Mac from your iPhone). This traffic stays on your local network, and your device asks for Local Network permission first.

Artist photos, artist info and lyrics come from your own server.

Apple

Apple handles purchases and downloads under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off at any time in your device's privacy settings.

Children

Paean isn't directed at children, and it doesn't collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.

Paean for Navidrome

Support

Paean plays the music on your own Navidrome server, on your iPhone and your Mac. One purchase gets you both.

Get in touch

Email support

paean-support@pm.me. Please include your device, its iOS or macOS version, the Paean version (shown in Settings) and your server's version.

What you need

  • A Navidrome server, or another Subsonic-compatible server, that you run yourself. Paean doesn't include any music or a streaming subscription.
  • An iPhone with iOS 16 or later, or a Mac with macOS 13 or later.

Getting started

Open Paean and enter your server's address, your user name and your password. The address includes http:// or https:// and the port if it has one, for example https://music.example.com or http://192.168.1.20:4533.

Common questions

Paean can't reach my server

  • Check the address, including http:// or https:// and the port.
  • Open the same address in a web browser on that device. If Navidrome doesn't load there, the server isn't reachable from that network.
  • If the server is on your home network, the device needs to be on that network too, or the server needs a way in from outside.

Artist photos, bios or lyrics are missing

These come from your server. Artist photos and bios need Navidrome's Last.fm agent turned on. Lyrics come from your files' tags or from .lrc files next to them.

Listening offline

Download albums or playlists, and they play without a connection. When Paean can't reach your server, it shows what's downloaded. On iPhone you can also turn on Go offline in Settings, for a flight or to save data.

Equalizer

Paean keeps a separate equalizer profile for each pair of headphones, so your speakers stay flat. On both iPhone and Mac you can make your own profiles, adjust bass and treble, fine-tune each band (its type, frequency, gain and width), or import a profile from AutoEQ.

Using your iPhone and Mac together

With both on the same Wi-Fi, each can play on the other or control it. Allow Local Network access when asked. If you said no, turn it on in Settings (iPhone) or System Settings (Mac), under Privacy & Security, then Local Network.

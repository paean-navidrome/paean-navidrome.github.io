Paean for Navidrome

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026. Applies to Paean on iPhone and Mac.

Paean doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app talks to the music server you choose, and what it keeps stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your server's address and your user name.
  • A sign-in token made from your password with a random salt, as the Subsonic protocol requires. Paean never stores your password itself.
  • Your settings, such as the theme, Home layout and equalizer profiles.
  • Music you download, a cache of recently played songs and cover art, and the last song you played, so the app can open where you left off.

None of this is sent anywhere but your own server. On iPhone, deleting the app removes all of it, including your downloads and settings. On Mac, macOS keeps app data after the app is deleted; to remove it, delete the Paean folder in ~/Library/Containers.

Who the app talks to

  • Your music server (Navidrome or another Subsonic-compatible server) that you sign in to. Paean uses it to browse, stream and download your music, and to save your likes, playlists, plays and play queue there. Your server's own policies apply to that data.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a free public directory of radio stations, when you use Radio. Paean sends it a station's name or address to look up the station's details and logo. Radio streams play directly from the station.
  • Your other devices running Paean on the same local network, if you use them together (for example, playing on your Mac from your iPhone). This stays on your local network, and your device asks for Local Network permission first.

Artist photos, artist info and lyrics come from your own server.

Apple

Purchases and downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send anonymous crash reports to the developer. You can turn that off at any time in your device's privacy settings.

Children

Paean isn't directed at children and doesn't collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

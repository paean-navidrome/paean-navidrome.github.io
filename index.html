Paean for Navidrome

Paean Support

Paean is a music player for iPhone and Mac that plays music from your own Navidrome server. A single purchase covers both the iPhone and Mac apps.

Contact us

Email paean-support@pm.me. Please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS or macOS version
  • Your Paean version (shown in Settings)
  • Your server's version

What you need

  • A Navidrome server, or another Subsonic-compatible server, that you host yourself. Paean doesn't include any music or a streaming subscription.
  • An iPhone running iOS 16 or later, or a Mac running macOS 13 or later.

Getting started

Open Paean and enter your server address, user name and password. The address must start with http:// or https:// and include the port number if your server uses one. For example: https://music.example.com or http://192.168.1.20:4533.

Common questions

Paean can't reach my server

  • Check that the address is correct, including http:// or https:// and the port number.
  • Open the same address in a web browser on the device where Paean is installed. If Navidrome doesn't load there, that device can't reach your server from its current network.
  • If your server is on your home network, connect your device to that network, or set up remote access to your server (for example, through a VPN or a reverse proxy).

Artist photos, bios or lyrics are missing

Paean gets these from your server.

  • Artist photos and bios: turn on the Last.fm agent in Navidrome.
  • Lyrics: Navidrome reads them from your music files' tags or from .lrc files stored next to the music files.

Listening offline

Download albums or playlists to play them without a connection. When Paean can't reach your server, it shows only your downloaded music. On iPhone, you can also turn on Go offline in Settings, for example on a flight or to save data.

Equalizer

Paean keeps a separate equalizer profile for each pair of headphones, so your speakers always play flat. On both iPhone and Mac, you can:

  • Create your own profiles
  • Adjust bass and treble
  • Fine-tune each band's type, frequency, gain and width
  • Import a profile from AutoEq

Using your iPhone and Mac together

When both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, each one can play music on the other or control the other's playback. Allow Local Network access when Paean asks. If you declined, turn it on later:

  • iPhone: Settings, then Paean, then Local Network
  • Mac: System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Local Network
